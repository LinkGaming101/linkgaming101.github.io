### Current Year: 998

In the common calendar of Khorvaire, days are 24 hours long, divided into day and night. Seven days make up a week, four weeks a month, and twelve months a year. The months correspond to the twelve moons of Eberron (see the Eberron Months table), and the prominent moon carries the name of the month in which its orbit brings it closest to the planet.

The seven days of the week, in order, are Sul, Mol, Zol, Wir, Zor, Far, Sar.

The common calendar of Khorvaire tracks the years since the founding of the kingdom of Galifar, using the abbreviation YK. The last king of Galifar, Jarot ir'Wynarn, died on 12 Therendor 894 YK. The Day of Mourning occurred a century later, on 20 Olarune 994 YK.

### Moons

#### Zarantyr, the Storm
![[Zarantyr.png]]
**Associated Month**: Zarantyr
**Associated Dragonmark**: Mark of Storm
**Description**: 
From Eberron, Zarantyr appears pearly white in coloration.
**Culture**: 
The shifter moonspeaker druids associate Zarantyr with silver metal, whereas shifter monks who follow the Way of the Shackled Beast associate white opals with the moon.
**Rumors & Legends**:
There is a popular superstition that there is a greater chance of being struck by lightning when Zarantyr is full, even when there are no clouds in the sky. 
There is a legend that one can travel to Zarantyr by finding a structure in Xen'drik known as the Siberys Staircase.

#### Olarune, the Sentinel
![[Olarune.png]]
**Associated Month**: Olarune
**Associated Dragonmark**: Mark of Sentinel
**Description**: 
From Eberron, Olarune appears pale orange in coloration. The edge of the moon resembles the rim of a shield.
**Rumors & Legends**:
According to shifter legends, lycanthropes originated when the spirit of the moon Olarune sought to create guardians of nature from shifters. However, the dark spirit of the thirteenth moon corrupted Olarune's blessing, creating lycanthropes.

#### Therendor, the Healer
![[Therendor.png]]
**Associated Month**: Therendor
**Associated Dragonmark**: Mark of Healing
**Description**: 
Therendor is a pale grey color in the night skies of Eberron. Therendor is sometimes referred to as the brother moon of Barrakas as they share a similar orbit. On occasions when they overlap or when Therendor eclipses Barrakas, Barrakas is described as hiding behind her older brother. 
**Culture**: 
Followers of the Path of Inspiration celebrate the Feast of the Founding when Therendor is a new moon in the third month of the year. 
The shifter moonspeaker druids consider Therendor to have governance over the passage of time.
**Rumors & Legends**:
Druids consider medicines to be more potent when created while Therendor is full. This belief is shared by the Seren.

#### Eyre, the Anvil
![[Eyre.png]]
**Associated Month**: Eyre
**Associated Dragonmark**: Mark of Making
**Description**: 
Eyre is a silvery grey color in the night skies of Eberron.

#### Dravago, the Herder
![[Dravago.png]]
**Associated Month**: Dravago
**Associated Dragonmark**: Mark of Handling
**Description**: 
Dravago has a pale lavender color in the night skies of Eberron.
**Rumors & Legends**:
Large in the sky, the lavender moon is easy to spot. Its orbit keeps it lagging behind the other moons and legend recounts that it is herding the other moons and keeping them in motion across the sky. 
Children born under Dravago are reportedly more attuned with plants and animals and often relate better to them than to people. They make gifted farmers, handlers, and herders, but are often awkward in social situations. 
Legend reports that stones fall from Dravago sometimes and landed in Eberron. These are called the "purple dust of Dravago" and alchemists have claimed they have latent magical potential.

#### Nymm, the Crown
![[Nymm.png]]
**Associated Month**: Nymm
**Associated Dragonmark**: Mark of Hospitality
**Description**: 
Nymm is a pale yellow color in the night skies of Eberron.
**Rumors & Legends**:
It is often associated with royalty. Being born when Nymm is ascendant is said to make one sociable and charismatic and to produce gifted orators or successful con artists. The halflings of House Ghallanda frequently offer discounted rates at their hostelries on nights when "King Nymm" is in its full moon phase.

#### Lharvion, the Eye
![[Lharvion.png]]
**Associated Month**: Lharvion
**Associated Dragonmark**: Mark of Detection
**Description**: 
Lharvion has a dull white color and features a 750‑mile-long (1,200‑kilometer) black chasm down the center such that, when viewed from Eberron at night, the full disk resembles a slitted eye.
**Rumors & Legends**:
Superstitions abound about this moon and weird events and disasters are thought to occur when Lharvion is full and the Eye is sighted.
Druids hold that children born under Lharvion are capable of supernatural awareness or insight. Talented scholars and diviners are commonly born in the month of Lharvion.

#### Barrakas, the Lantern
![[Barrakas.png]]
**Associated Month**: Barrakas
**Associated Dragonmark**: Mark of Finding
**Description**: 
Barrakas is a Bright gray color in the night skies of Eberron
**Rumors & Legends**:
Barrakas is the brightest of Eberron's moons, and hunters value its light when stalking prey at night. This "sister moon" of Therendor shares a similar (if wider) orbit and occasionally seems to hide behind her closer brother. On nights when Therendor eclipses Barrakas—"when Barrakas is shy"—sailors worry about getting lost at sea. Those born when Barrakas is ascendant are thought to be gifted hunters, but many also believe they have a knack for clarifying things that others find confusing. The son of Lharvion sees what others cannot see, while the daughter of Barrakas sheds light on that which is already in the open.

#### Rhaan, the Book
![[Rhaan.png]]
**Associated Month**: Rhaan
**Associated Dragonmark**: Mark of Scribing
**Description**: 
Rhaan is a Pale blue color in the night skies of Eberron
**Rumors & Legends**:
The smallest of Eberron's moons, Rhaan looks more like a bright star. When viewed with a spyglass, one may see a series of ridges that vaguely resemble scribblings on a page. The druids say that Rhaan empowers creative thought, and they believe that dancers, musicians, poets, and artists of all stripes draw inspiration from the Book.

#### Sypheros, the Shadow
![[Sypheros.png]]
**Associated Month**: Sypheros
**Associated Dragonmark**: Mark of Shadow
**Description**: 
Sypheros is a Smoky gray color in the night skies of Eberron
**Rumors & Legends**:
Sypheros is a dim moon, and people often find it difficult to spot in the night sky. Recent observations using powerful spyglasses have confirmed that a jagged crack runs down the center of the moon, as if the moon is splitting in two. Many believe that children born in the month of Sypheros inherit a shifty, untrustworthy nature, and parents often seek to time pregnancies to avoid this month. However, the druids say that the children of Sypheros are not evil; rather, they are comfortable with the shadows and can face them without fear.

#### Aryth, the Gateway
![[Aryth.png]]
**Associated Month**: Aryth
**Associated Dragonmark**: Mark of Passage
**Description**: 
Aryth is a Orange-red color in the night skies of Eberron
**Rumors & Legends**:
The dragon Vvaraak told the first druids that Aryth has a similar effect on manifest zones as Zarantyr has on tides, and that particularly weak manifest zones appear only when Aryth is in its full moon phase. Some say that those born under this moon are restless folk, driven to travel and explore; Lhazaar, who led the first great migration from Sarlona to Khorvaire, is said to have been a child of Aryth.

#### Vult, the Warder
![[Vult.png]]
**Associated Month**: Vult
**Associated Dragonmark**: Mark of Warding
**Description**: 
Vult is a Pockmarked steel gray color in the night skies of Eberron
**Rumors & Legends**:
Vult is the farthest moon from Eberron, and some legends say that it holds back forces that lurk out within the stars. The druids say that children of Vult are practical, careful folk who generally plan ahead and prepare for the worst; when disaster strikes, the children of Vult hold civilization together. In his study of the Serens, the sage Galeoin reported that the barbarians believe that Vult "devours" the spirits of deceased greatwyrms that fail to attain godhood.
